# Kim Soohyeong Personal Page - Design Direction

## Theme: Quiet Midnight

- Base: nearly black charcoal with deep navy surfaces
- Mood: calm, focused, and understated rather than decorative
- Typography: large clear Korean headings with restrained small labels
- Layout: generous spacing, thin borders, and one cool-blue accent
- Image: circular profile photo as the only visual focal point

## Rules

- No bright gradients, flashy motion, or decorative illustrations
- Keep reading comfortable on both desktop and mobile
- Use the blue accent only for links, labels, and small emphasis
